# Code of Conduct

Glyt is a project for people who want to make things together.

Treat contributors as people, not resources.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful.
- Be curious before being dismissive.
- Disagree with ideas without degrading people.
- Give useful explanations.
- Admit uncertainty.
- Give credit where credit is due.
- Accept correction without turning technical discussion into personal conflict.
- Make room for different kinds of expertise.
- Do not intentionally make the project harder for others to enter.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, targeted abuse, threats, deliberate sabotage, discriminatory behavior, bad-faith manipulation, or repeated hostile conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project healthy.

The goal is not punishment. The goal is to keep the workshop usable for everyone.
