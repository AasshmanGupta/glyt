# Decision Log

This document records important project decisions so contributors can understand not only what Glyt currently says, but why.

## D001 — North Star

**Decision:** “Advancement without losing soul.”

**Reason:** Glyt should increase capability without forcing the human creator to become machine-like.

## D002 — Universal ambition

**Decision:** Glyt aims at everything rather than selecting a permanent list of domains.

**Reason:** A domain list inevitably becomes a ceiling.

## D003 — One underlying language

**Decision:** Glyt should remain one coherent language and grammar while supporting domain vocabulary.

**Reason:** Domain-specific grammars would fragment knowledge and make the same concepts behave differently depending on context.

## D004 — Syntax is not finalized in v0.1.0

**Decision:** Do not lock in concrete syntax merely to appear complete.

**Reason:** The project does not yet have sufficient evidence to know what syntax best serves its goals.

## D005 — Explorer and Achiever are intent categories

**Decision:** Do not organize the experience primarily around beginner/expert status.

**Reason:** People of any skill level may want either discovery or immediate results.

## D006 — Learning goal

**Decision:** Glyt should naturally teach users enough underlying structure that they become independently capable.

**Reason:** Tool dependency is not the desired outcome.

## D007 — Glyt and Loom are separate

**Decision:** Glyt defines the language. Loom is a separate universal transformation engine.

**Reason:** The language's identity should not be coupled to one transformation implementation.

## D008 — Community can overturn the founder

**Decision:** Evidence can justify changing even foundational principles.

**Reason:** The goal is to discover the best design, not preserve authorship as authority.
