# Domain Model

Glyt is intentionally universal.

“Universal” means more than supporting today's familiar list of domains.

## One core, many vocabularies

The underlying language should remain one language.

Its surface experience may adapt through domain vocabulary and libraries.

A musician may work with concepts such as tracks, notes, tempo, timing, and effects.
A game creator may work with scenes, entities, physics, input, and worlds.
A scientist may work with models, matrices, simulations, samples, and measurements.

These experiences can feel dramatically different without becoming unrelated grammars.

## Why one language?

The project values:

- one coherent mental model
- reduced context switching
- reusable knowledge across domains
- a single trusted creative home
- the ability to combine previously separated capabilities

## Domain extensions

Domain vocabulary should extend what the language can express rather than replace the language's grammar.

Users should also be able to build their own domain vocabulary.

## Imported languages

Interoperability should remain possible.

A user who prefers another language for part of a project should not have to pretend that preference does not exist.

Glyt remains Glyt while communicating with the surrounding ecosystem.
