# Research Agenda

Glyt should be discovered through evidence rather than invented entirely from one person's intuition.

## Research themes

### Programming education

Investigate how learners move from memorization to genuine conceptual understanding.

### Human-computer interaction

Study how people express goals, recover from errors, discover capabilities, and maintain agency in creative systems.

### Existing languages

Continue studying languages not to copy syntax, but to identify ideas worth evolving and failure modes worth avoiding.

### Accessibility

Investigate how different cognitive, sensory, educational, and experiential backgrounds affect programming tools.

### Domain design

Study how a single underlying system can develop domain-specific vocabulary without becoming fragmented.

### Safety

Investigate capability-based security, sandboxing, isolation, rollback, resource limits, and safe experimentation.

### Tooling

Study environments that make creation, debugging, packaging, and distribution feel coherent rather than fragmented.

## Working rule

For major design decisions, distinguish:

- evidence
- observation
- hypothesis
- personal preference
- experiment
- established project principle

This prevents aesthetic preference from accidentally becoming constitutional law.

## Open research questions

- What is the smallest useful conceptual core?
- Which conveniences can be derived instead of built into the core?
- What makes a syntax feel understandable rather than merely familiar?
- How can the same grammar support radically different domains?
- How should vague intent be expressed without pretending a language can read minds?
- What kinds of diagnostics most effectively teach rather than merely report errors?
- How can high-level convenience and deep machine control coexist without turning into separate worlds?
- What makes programming exploration feel alive without becoming attention manipulation?
- How can safe environments expose powerful capabilities for learning without granting uncontrolled access?
