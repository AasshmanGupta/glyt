# Terminology

## Glyt

The universal programming language project.

## Creator

Anyone using or contributing to Glyt to make, understand, explore, or improve something.

## Explorer

A creator whose immediate intention emphasizes discovery, experimentation, learning, or novelty.

## Achiever

A creator whose immediate intention emphasizes reaching a known outcome efficiently.

Explorer and Achiever describe intent, not expertise.

## Core

The smallest conceptual foundation that should underpin the language.

## Derived capability

Functionality built from the core rather than requiring a new fundamental language mechanism.

## Domain vocabulary

Words, libraries, abstractions, or concepts that make a particular field feel native while preserving Glyt's underlying grammar.

## Depth

Increasingly direct understanding and control of underlying computation.

## Dead friction

Difficulty that consumes effort without meaningfully improving understanding, safety, capability, or creation.

## Soul

The human creator and their agency, curiosity, intent, and ability to make things.

## Loom

A separate universal transformation/build/translation engine. Loom is not Glyt.
